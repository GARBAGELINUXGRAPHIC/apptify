## Home showcase gradients

Retrieved 2026-11-18. These are source images, with no recoloring or generated replacements. Light and dark themes use the same assets.

The four `openai-*.webp` files come from [OpenAI Signals](https://openai.com/signals/). The official image CDN supplied 960px WebP versions at quality 90 (`?w=960&q=90&fm=webp`):

- `gradients/openai-blue-pink.webp`: [original image](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/h2hw4nJmey9JslWgcmhwO/579de2981473fb54e5b3bc9a18e55e40/how-work-is-rebundling-in-the-ai-age-cover.png), “Work at the Frontier: AI’s effect on freelance work”.
- `gradients/openai-green-purple.webp`: [original image](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/6QhDGDocz7QOwfPndw4sCT/5681abf3a9b839cccf7c108221d23fcf/how-workers-use-chatgpt-cover.png), “ChatGPT’s expanding world of work”.
- `gradients/openai-purple-blue.webp`: [original image](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/2grKxXJHSpaLubgpwbsSx5/d1c06eb9de4c77392cc60e74b048e8e2/the-eus-next-chapter-in-ai-is-agentic-cover.png), “The EU’s next chapter in AI is agentic”.
- `gradients/openai-cyan-blue.webp`: [original image](https://images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/YVOmB2UlP6HNIxMKNnpu1/1e3800b890b91728f782309564187b46/1lmaflspzj765gaje1hbon-cover.png), “How workers are unlocking new ways of working”.

The two `wang-*.jpg` files are unmodified images published by Justin Jay Wang in [Methods for random gradients](https://justinjay.wang/methods-for-random-gradients/), also shown in the user's visual reference. They are the author's DALL·E 2 experiments, not claimed to have been used on the OpenAI website:

- `gradients/wang-pink.jpg`: [dreamlike-0.jpg](https://storage.ghost.io/c/e5/51/e5518248-64c6-46dd-941d-a7fd73d59c60/content/images/2024/11/dreamlike-0.jpg).
- `gradients/wang-peach.jpg`: [dreamlike-5.jpg](https://storage.ghost.io/c/e5/51/e5518248-64c6-46dd-941d-a7fd73d59c60/content/images/2024/11/dreamlike-5.jpg).
